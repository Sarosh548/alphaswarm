Terms of Service

Draft - plain-language terms for the AlphaSwarm paper-trading platform. Last updated: 2026-11-19.

1. What AlphaSwarm is

AlphaSwarm is a paper-trading platform. Software bots analyse public crypto market data and place simulated trades with virtual money. No real money is traded, no real orders are placed on any exchange, and the platform never asks for, stores, or uses exchange API keys or wallet keys.

2. No financial advice, no profit guarantee

Nothing on this platform is financial advice. Balances, profit/loss figures, win rates and every other result are simulated paper results. Past paper performance is not a promise of real returns. Bots can and do lose paper money; a bot that performed well in simulation may perform badly in the future or in real markets. Do not make financial decisions based on this platform.

3. Accounts

You are responsible for your account and for keeping your password private. The first registered account is the platform administrator. We may pause or remove accounts that abuse the service or attempt to disrupt it.

4. Plans and billing (TEST MODE)

Billing currently runs in test mode: no real payment provider is connected, no card is ever really charged, and any test card number succeeds. Plan prices shown are examples of the intended pricing. If real billing is introduced later, it will be announced in advance and these terms will be updated before any real charge can happen.

5. Availability and changes

The platform is provided "as is", without any warranty that it will be available, uninterrupted, or error-free. Market data comes from public third-party sources and may be delayed or unavailable. We may change, suspend, or stop features at any time; strategies and results shown may change as the software is improved.

6. Acceptable use

Do not attempt to break, overload, or gain unauthorised access to the platform or other users' data. Paper-trading results must not be marketed or resold as proof of real trading performance.

Privacy Policy

Draft - plain-language policy for the AlphaSwarm paper-trading platform. Last updated: 2026-11-19.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, and a salted, hashed form of your password (we never store your password in plain text and cannot read it).
  • Paper-trading activity: the bots, settings, and simulated trades created under your account, and your plan choice.
  • Test-mode billing records: the plan, the amount, and only the last 4 digits of the test card used. We never receive or store full card numbers, and in test mode no payment is processed at all.
  • Basic technical logs: server access logs (page and API requests with timestamps), kept for a limited time to run and protect the service.

2. What we never collect

We do not collect exchange API keys, wallet private keys, real payment card details, or government ID. The platform is paper-trading only and has no use for them.

3. How your data is used

Only to run the platform: signing you in, showing your bots and paper results, enforcing plan features, keeping backups, and preventing abuse. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

4. Cookies

We use one session cookie to keep you signed in. There are no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

5. Backups and retention

Platform data is backed up daily and backups are kept for a short rolling period (7 days), after which they are deleted. If you ask us to delete your account, we will remove your account data from the live platform; copies inside recent backups disappear when those backups age out.

6. Your choices

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion of your account at any time via the contact below.
